# FEA & CFD Simulations

## Overview
Visual results detailing structural integrity, tooth contact stresses, mesh convergence, and oil-splash lubrication dynamics.

## Visual Assets
* `von-mises-comparison.png`: Side-by-side Von Mises stress distribution plots comparing gear tooth roots under peak load.
* `contact-stress-mesh.png`: Contact pressure distribution and Hertzian surface stress maps along the tooth mesh line.
* `fluid-flow-streamlines.png`: CFD velocity vectors showing gearbox casing oil churn and splash efficiency.

## Analysis Setup
* **FEA Boundary Conditions:** Fixed planet carrier shafts; torque applied to sun gear input.
* **CFD Physics:** Multiphase (Air/Oil) VoF (Volume of Fluid) rotating mesh domain.
